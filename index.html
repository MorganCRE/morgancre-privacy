Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when submitting a rental inquiry through third-party listing platforms including Zillow, Apartments.com, Apartment List, Rent.com, and Zumper. This information may include your name, phone number, email address, and rental preferences.

How We Use Your Information

We use your contact information solely to respond to your rental inquiry, provide information about available properties, and follow up regarding your interest in renting with us. We do not sell, share, or distribute your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS Messaging

By submitting a rental inquiry that includes your phone number, you consent to receive SMS messages from Morgan CRE regarding your inquiry. Message frequency varies — typically 2 to 3 initial messages and up to 3 follow-up messages. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages. Reply HELP for assistance.Opt-in evidence and consent flow documentation is available at: https://morgancre.github.io/morgancre-privacy/opt-in-evidence

Data Retention

We retain your contact information for the duration of the leasing process and for up to one year thereafter for record-keeping purposes.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact us at sophia@morgancre.com.

Terms of Service

Program Description

Morgan CRE Property Management operates an automated SMS messaging program to respond to prospective tenant inquiries submitted through rental listing platforms.

Message Frequency

After submitting a rental inquiry, you may receive up to 6 SMS messages: 2 to 3 initial information messages and up to 3 follow-up messages if no response is received.

Message & Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-Out Instructions

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe from further SMS communications. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.

Help

Reply HELP for help or contact us at sophia@morgancre.com.

Supported Carriers

Service is available on all major US carriers. Carrier is not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
