SMS Opt-In Evidence

Morgan CRE Property Management
This page documents how prospective tenants provide consent to receive SMS messages from Morgan CRE.

Opt-In Path 1: Rental Listing Platform Inquiry

Prospective tenants submit a rental inquiry form on third-party listing platforms including Zillow, Apartments.com, Apartment List, Rent.com, and Zumper. By entering their phone number and submitting the inquiry form, they request contact from the property manager and consent to receive SMS messages regarding their inquiry.

Each platform's terms of service informs users they may be contacted by the property owner or manager via phone, email, or text message when submitting an inquiry.

Consent action: Submitting the inquiry form with a phone number constitutes express consent to receive SMS from Morgan CRE about their rental inquiry.

Disclosures presented at opt-in: Message frequency varies (typically 2–3 initial messages plus up to 3 follow-ups). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

Opt-In Path 2: Direct Text from For Rent Sign

Prospective tenants initiate SMS contact by texting our leasing number after seeing it displayed on a For Rent sign at one of our properties. By texting us first, they provide express written consent to receive an automated availability reply and follow-up messages from Morgan CRE.

For Rent sign displaying our leasing number (representative example):

🏠 FOR RENT
Silverlake Garden Apartments
1BR & 2BR Units Available
Silver Lake, Los Angeles

Text: (323) 688-5066
for availability & info
Disclosure presented at point of contact:
By texting (323) 688-5066, you consent to receive automated SMS messages from Morgan CRE Property Management regarding rental availability. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Terms & Privacy: https://morgancre.github.io/morgancre-privacy

Opt-Out Handling

Every outbound message includes "Reply STOP to opt out." When a recipient replies STOP, they are immediately removed from all further automated messaging. A confirmation message is sent acknowledging the opt-out. No further messages are sent after opt-out.

STOP confirmation message: "You have been unsubscribed from Morgan CRE messages. No further messages will be sent. Reply START to resubscribe."

HELP response: "Morgan CRE leasing info. For assistance contact sophia@morgancre.com. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."

Privacy Policy & Terms: https://morgancre.github.io/morgancre-privacy
